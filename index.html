What the extension does

Cookie Shield injects a script into web pages that looks for cookie consent banners and clicks the reject or "necessary only" option on your behalf. It then removes the banner element from the page.

This process happens entirely within your browser using standard browser extension APIs. No information about which pages you visit, which banners were found, or what was clicked is ever transmitted anywhere.

The only data stored is a local banner count shown in the extension popup. This number lives in your browser's local storage, never leaves your device, and is erased when you remove the extension.

Permissions explained

Cookie Shield requests access to all websites. This is required for the extension to detect and dismiss cookie banners regardless of which site you're visiting. This permission does not grant the ability to read page content beyond what is needed to find and click consent buttons — and nothing read is ever stored or transmitted.

The storage permission is used solely to save your enabled/disabled preference and the local banner count between browser sessions.

Third parties

Cookie Shield shares no data with any third party. There are no advertising integrations, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no external dependencies that make network requests.

Children's privacy

Cookie Shield does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. There is no data collection of any kind regardless of the user's age.

Changes to this policy
