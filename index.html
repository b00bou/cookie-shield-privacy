What Cookie Shield does

Cookie Shield is a browser extension that automatically clicks the reject button on cookie consent banners. It runs entirely within your browser using your device's processing power. No data leaves your device at any point.

Data we collect

None. Cookie Shield does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal data, browsing history, or any other information about you or your device.

The extension stores one number locally in your browser — a count of how many banners have been rejected — using your browser's built-in local storage. This number never leaves your device.

Permissions explained

Cookie Shield requests the following browser permissions:

  • Access to all websites — required to detect and interact with cookie banners on any site you visit. We read the page only to find and click reject buttons.
  • Storage — used solely to save your rejected-banner count locally in your browser.
  • Active tab / scripting — required to run the banner-detection code on the current page when you click the extension icon.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data.

Third parties

Cookie Shield does not use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. There are no third parties involved in any aspect of the extension.

Children's privacy

Cookie Shield does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. There is nothing to collect.
